Privacy Policy

After You. Last updated November 7, 2026.

After You is a private journal. We don't collect your data, and we can't read what you write.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we run no server for it. Nothing you put in the app is sent to us.

Where your journal is stored

  • On your device. Your pages, recordings and photos are stored in the app on your device.
  • In your own iCloud. If iCloud is on, they sync between your devices through your private iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy. They use your iCloud storage, and we have no access to them.

Your writing, recordings and photos are end-to-end encrypted in iCloud, with keys held in your iCloud Keychain, so neither we nor Apple can read them. iCloud can still see when something was added or changed, and how large it is. A single entry over about a million characters is protected by iCloud's standard encryption instead. If you lose access to iCloud Keychain and all its recovery options, the iCloud copy can't be read, so keep an export.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app uses the microphone only when you tap Speak. Your speech is turned into text on your device, and is not sent to us or, by this app, to Apple's speech servers. If your device can't do this for your language, the recording is still saved without words.

Camera and photos

The camera and your photos are used only when you choose to add a picture to a page. Only the pictures you choose are stored in the journal.

Password and Face ID

The optional password is stored only as a scrambled version in your iCloud Keychain, never as the password itself. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by your device, and the app never sees your biometric data. The password keeps the app closed; it doesn't encrypt your pages.

Exports

An export is a file you create and keep wherever you choose. It isn't protected by your password, so keep it somewhere safe.

Deleting your data

To delete one day, hold it in Pages (right-click on Mac) and choose Delete This Page. To delete your whole journal, go to Settings and choose Delete everything. Both remove it from your device and from iCloud on all your devices, and can't be undone.

Deleting the app removes only its copy on that device, and any copy already in iCloud stays. Anything not yet in iCloud or an export is lost when the app is deleted. So to remove your journal completely, use Delete everything before deleting the app.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
